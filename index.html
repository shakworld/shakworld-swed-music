A CLEARER MODEL

Your music earns.
You deserve your share.

SWED's proposed royalty model gives 80% of distributable royalties to the artist and retains 20% for SWED. No subscription fee is planned for distribution.

Final royalty calculations, deductions, reporting schedules, payout thresholds and terms must be defined in the artist agreement before launch.
